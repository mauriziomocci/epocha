Examples of future investigations the user wants to launch (2026-10-29),
"solo esempi ma rendono l'idea": (1) the Axis wins WWII because it reaches the
nuclear weapon first — how would events have changed up to today; (2) the 6
January 2021 Capitol assault succeeds and Trump takes power by a coup — what
would the world look like and which events would it have triggered.

(3) Prospective, not counterfactual: what will happen to the economy with the
advent of AI leading to AGI. No retrodiction of the outcome is possible; the
mechanisms are validated instead against past technological transitions
(industrial revolution, electrification, recent automation). Candidate
literature, to verify before use: the task-based model of automation
(Acemoglu & Restrepo), AI in growth models (Aghion, Jones & Jones), explicit
scenarios for the transition to AGI (Korinek & Suh). Needs the technology
phase and a task-based labour market on top of the existing economy.

**Why:** counterfactual history is the most ambitious use of the psychohistory
goal ([[project-vision-psychohistory]]): change one event at a real historical
point and study how the possible futures diverge.

**How to apply:**
- Historical initial state built from sources Epocha may use
  ([[feedback-ethics-first]]), via the knowledge graph.
- Retrodiction first: from the same point, the factual branch must broadly
  reproduce what really happened before the counterfactual branch is trusted.
- Needs phases not yet opened: military and conflict, diplomacy among
  civilizations, technology (nuclear) for (1); crowds and media for (2), on
  top of the existing political institutions and factions.
- Outputs as distributions of trajectories with their driving mechanisms,
  never a single narrative presented as fact.
- Ethics for scenarios with real living people: model institutional and social
  dynamics, never make the real persons speak or act as themselves; label every
  result as hypothetical and conditional on the model's assumptions. Agreed
  with the user (2026-10-29): such scenarios are fully legitimate research;
  in the model real people become ROLES (an executive refusing the election
  result, the courts, the army, the states, the allies), and the study is of
  what that configuration produces, never of what a real person would say.
