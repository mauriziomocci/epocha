Three inherited premises died on the measurement bench: the truncated normal does not fail the dispersion criterion once the residual is corrected per kinship branch; the observed-scale slope attenuation is not a cost of the logit route, since both families attenuate 7-9% away from centre; and the boundary-mass argument that then carried the logit collapsed in round 2, because the parameter space it was bought for is closed by the amendment's own declared values. Phase 2.1 is done, and it is the defect the whole work item exists for. The polygenic kernel wrote its residual as (1 - h2), a weighted average whose coefficients sum to one rather than a variance decomposition, so the population settled at 48.85% of its declared era amplitude at h2 = 0.55 and lost between 21% and 51% across the shipped range — every result depending on the variance of a heritable character was uninterpretable. The three residual scales now come from the variance identity, the single-parent signal coefficient is halved to what the parent-offspring regression implies, and the signal acts on the parent's deviation from the era mean so an off-centre era reproduces its own mean. The criterion is exact rather than sampled, because no band on realized amplitude separates the correct model from the defective one — that one lands at 95.82% on the single-parent branch, inside any tolerance wide enough for Monte Carlo noise. Eight mutations go red, including the h2**4 trap that leaves the regression slope correct while inflating the variance by 12.43%. Phase 2.2 is done, and it caught the same defect one level up. Phase 1 shipped the per-era parameter sections and the validator; nothing proved the values were then read. Every synthetic template in the suite declared the same numbers the code fell back to, so an implementation ignoring the sections passed everything. Tests declaring values that differ from the fallbacks found the trait path already wired and the education path not wired at all: it read a key the amendment had removed from the schema, so the loader rejects any template carrying it and the read always resolved to a hardcoded constant — a dead read wearing a live default. Phase 2.3 is done, both halves. Education stopped being a deterministic contraction, whose fixed point is not a reduced dispersion but zero — measured at 0.00004 of a 0.150 starting spread after eight generations at the highest shipped coefficient, and exact zero at the lowest — and now goes through the same kernel as the traits rather than a reimplementation beside it. The Clark rule got its innovation: it shipped as a pure 70/30 weighted average, which is a model with the opposite asymptotic behaviour to the AR(1) it cites, and measured mobility of exactly 0.0000 with a parent-child rank correlation of exactly 1.0000 from the second generation. The amplitude is solved, not read off the stationary identity: rounding to five integer labels and clamping at both ends are non-linear, so the continuous-scale answer overshoots the declared dispersion by 2.26%. Two blind spots surfaced and became rules — a probe that declares the same value as the fallback cannot tell a template read from a constant, and a mutation aimed at a constant can land on the docstring that names it. Adding the draw shifted the shared RNG stream and exposed a latent flake in the MVP integration test, whose expected memory count held only for some agent placements: world/generator.py:172 places agents with the unseeded global random, so the wealth order — and through it the social-mobility memories that interrupt a dedup streak — depends on how much randomness the rest of the suite consumed first. The test now asserts the invariant dedup actually guarantees. No seed was changed to make anything pass. Phase 2.4 is done, and it is the one where the amendment itself was the defect. A4 already discharged the requirement in prose, so the temptation was to ship nothing — but it published two crossing thresholds described as measured on the truncated family that nothing in the repository could regenerate: the only stationary solver convolved the marginal with itself, which is the sum of two independent draws by construction, and had no correlation parameter to turn. A12 carried the same two measurements with different values. A project aiming at a paper cannot publish a measured number nobody can reproduce, so the instrument now exists: a Gaussian copula on the current marginal, reducing to the independent solver at zero to 1e-9 and cross-checked against a naive Monte Carlo bench. Regenerated, the crossings are 0.5403 and 0.7122 against the 0.53/0.75 and 0.54/0.74 the two sections carried — and truncation inverts the order, because the off-centre education pair must first make back the 2.28% the clamp costs it. The mating convention is now declared instead of left to whoever implements it. Two further corrections came out of the same pass: a deliberation note claimed the homogamy score does not weight a transmitted character, which couple.py:87 contradicts, and a stationary-amplitude figure this branch had published days earlier — 91.8% at the modal configuration — measures 99.91%. Phase 3 is done. The Quranic spousal share is gendered — Q4:12 gives a widower 1/2 childless and 1/4 with children where the code applied the widow's 1/4 and 1/8 to both, so a surviving husband received half his entitlement. Estate tax and remainder now conserve exactly rather than to an ulp: the smaller term is derived by subtraction on either side of a rate of one half, which is Sterbenz's condition, and the guarantee names its summation order. The first version of that test was tautological — it re-derived the tax inside the test and survived a mutant deriving the wrong term; reading the tax from the treasury instead kills four. And the education coefficients now trace to a source that exists: the shipped values rested on an attribution to Chetty et al. (2014) that reports no education-persistence coefficient of any value, and the five templates ship 0.60 four times and 0.30 for sci-fi, anchored on Black and Devereux (2011) Table 3 — where the midparent-comparable figure is the SUM of two per-parent 0.30s, not one of them. Both whitepapers' §4.1.4 changed from a list of open defects to a list of corrections with what each replaced, plus an explicit statement that results produced before and after are not comparable. Phase 4 is done. The migration gain stopped subtracting a count of ticks from a money-per-tick and became a discounted present value. The sources separate cleanly and the module had cited the wrong one: Harris-Todaro is a ONE-PERIOD equality and cannot license a horizon, Todaro 1969 supplies the present-value structure, Sjaastad 1962 the horizon and the cost definition. The end of working life is derived rather than chosen — 62 is the single age from which both horizons Sjaastad publishes fall out of his own bracket midpoints — and the annuity reproduces the two factors he prints on page 89. The correction costs something and it is stated: the break-even distance moves from 4.8 ticks to 220.5, distance effectively stops binding, and the source says as much itself. Zone stability is now reported once and named a simulation value instead of being copied into every destination, which is what told a language model it was comparing zones on a dimension where they are identical. Mutation caught another unfailable test: the tick-duration conversion was asserted by hand-computing it inside the test, so an implementation hardcoding 365 passed. And rebuilding the trapped-agent fixtures surfaced a real limit now documented — only the earnings-foregone half of Sjaastad's cost is modelled, so an agent whose zone has no wage data travels for free. Phase 5 is done and the phase-6 code audit has run. Its first verdict was NOT CONVERGED on eighteen findings, and the five blocking ones are worth naming because none is a formula error: the docstring of inherit_trait — the function this whole work item exists for — still described the defect as current and its own correction as "CONTESTED ... NOT resolved here"; the present-value horizon reaching zero at 62 makes an agent permanently immobile at any wage differential, which is arithmetically correct and was declared nowhere; the whitepapers contradicted themselves between §4.1.5 and §11 in both languages; and two acceptance criteria had no witness at all. Three more tests were found unable to fail — one compared this file's own helper against a literal without ever calling the kernel — which brings the count of criteria-that-cannot-fail found on this work item to seven. All eighteen were closed, and the audit was re-run twice more. Round 2 returned NOT CONVERGED on nine, and its blocking finding was self-inflicted: round 1's own remediation had promoted a citation the amendment declares unverified into an asserted one, and attached one chapter's title to another. Round 3 caught the same class a third time — a section heading inside chapter 8 promoted to that chapter's title, published in five files by the very sentence certifying it verified. The fix that closes the CLASS rather than the instance is to stop writing titles and cite chapter numbers only, which is now the rule across all seven artifacts. Round 3 also found two guards that could not fail for a structural reason worth recording: the copula's asymmetry is invisible through the only observable the module exposed, because the distribution of a SUM is invariant under transposing the joint, so the joint itself is now exposed and read by the test; and the cache-mutation guard exercised one of the birth path's three passes while its comment claimed all three. Thirteen more findings are closed. Round 4 caught the citation a fourth time, and that is the finding worth keeping: each remediation had corrected the occurrences its author remembered rather than the ones that exist, and the fourth pass missed two inside the very module that had just written the no-titles rule. The fix is no longer a fifth hand pass but a structural guard — a test that reads every line of the repository and fails if any of them names a chapter of that source outside the sanctioned range or pairs it with a title. It fired on its first run and found seven more occurrences, including both bibliographies and an English design spec nobody had opened. The count of criteria-that-cannot-fail found on this work item stands at thirteen, which is the single most useful number it has produced, and the guard is the first thing built to stop one class of them recurring rather than to fix an instance. The merge stays where the plan puts it, after the phase-6 audit on the code.

The adversarial audit took four rounds, and it earned every one. Round 1 returned twenty-two findings against a suite that was already green at 308 tests — the worst being that Gaussian noise in the social-mobility rule pushed newborns past the end of the class ladder into the enslaved class, a quarter of the children of poor parents in a poor zone under the modern-democracy template, with no enslaved parent anywhere. Round 2 found that two of round 1's own fixes had introduced defects worse than what they repaired: a crash that rolled back an entire tick's inheritance batch, and a repair that reduced a spec-mandated propagation to zero in the famine scenario it was written for. Round 3 found that a test reported as closed multiplied by zero the very coefficient it claimed to pin. Round 4 broke the pattern — every functional mutation goes red, nothing escapes — and returned CONVERGED on 2026-08-05. Along the way the suite grew from 183 to 372 demography tests, and the remediation fixed mental health that was never inherited because the templates named a field that does not exist, loans that left a nationalized estate's credit book with the family, estate tax credited to a currency no spending path reads, a wage window that overstated steadily-employed zones by 20%, and a famine that generated a quarter of a million memory rows in a single tick. The verdict covers the code as scoped — not the eight DESIGN defects the code implements faithfully, which went to a separate work item with its own phase-2 gate. That work item is now open, and its own gate raised the count from eight to ten. Chief among them: the polygenic kernel is not variance-preserving, so trait diversity collapses to 48.8% of the declared era distribution and every simulated society drifts toward homogeneity — and the collapse is not a corner case of the highest heritability, it costs between 21% and 51% across the 0.22–0.55 range the five templates actually ship. All eight are documented in whitepaper §4.1 as currently true of the model, with their measured magnitudes, rather than deferred silently; the two the later gate added are not, and §4.1 and §6.2 must be rewritten when they cease to be true.
